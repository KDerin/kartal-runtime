# Code of Conduct

KARTAL Runtime is committed to an open, rigorous, and respectful research and
engineering community.

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Give actionable, technically grounded feedback.
- Credit prior work and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Respect privacy, security boundaries, and responsible disclosure.
- Welcome contributors regardless of background, identity, experience, or
  institutional affiliation.
- Clearly distinguish measured results from hypotheses or promotional claims.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, plagiarism, deliberate
misrepresentation of results, and irresponsible disclosure of vulnerabilities
are not tolerated.

## Reporting and enforcement

For security-sensitive reports, use the repository's private security advisory
channel. For other conduct concerns, contact the maintainer privately through a
verified professional channel listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile.

The maintainer may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community and the integrity of the project.

## Scope

This code applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
project events, and public representation of KARTAL Runtime.
